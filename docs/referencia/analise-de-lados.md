# The side analysis

> Moved out of `CLAUDE.md` on 04/11/2026, verbatim, so that the context every session and worker loads stays small. `CLAUDE.md` keeps a short summary under the same heading; when this file changes in a way that makes that summary wrong, fix the summary too.

The analysis asks the LLM for the question in dispute within the story ("questão": a single question that the actors answer in opposite ways, such as "A empresa descumpriu o contrato?") and for the sides. A side ("lado") is one answer to that question, held by named actors: accusation against defence, decision against criticism, statement against denial. Every actor who gives the same answer is on the same side, even with different arguments, never one side per actor, and a third side only exists when its answer opposes the other two. Complementary information, different details or the mere sequence of events do not make a dispute, and in doubt the story has no question and one side. The question is about the new fact of the pauta, what the texts report as having happened now; earlier episodes that the texts recap are context. If the question of the new fact has only one side with a named actor, the prompt tells the model to look, about the same fact, for another question on which named actors take opposite positions (in the prompt only: there is no extra call). `Summarizer.preparar()` runs the analysis for every pauta, single-outlet ones included, at temperature 0.1, on the whole texts, each with its outlet and title. The answer is a JSON object whose only required key is `lados`; the question comes first so the model states the dispute, then the answer of each named actor with a literal excerpt that proves it, and only then the sides:

```json
{"questao": "one question, or empty", "respostas_por_ator": [{"ator": "Construtora Alfa", "resposta": "Não", "trecho": "literal excerpt", "veiculo": "Jornal do Vale"}], "lados": [{"resposta": "Não", "posicao": "one sentence", "atores": ["Construtora Alfa"], "grupos": [], "trecho": "only in a caveat: literal excerpt", "veiculos": ["Jornal do Vale"]}], "motivo": "one sentence"}
```

Only a position with a named actor counts as a side. `atores` are names or specific designations ("Construtora Alfa", "gestão de Duarte", "governo federal"), in the form the texts use most ("Duarte", not the full name). `grupos` are unnamed collectives ("economistas", "especialistas") and vague designations with no qualifier ("o governo", "a empresa"). A position held only by groups is a caveat ("ressalva"): it does not count as a side, gets no analyst, and enters the episode as an attributed question asked by Helena.

`lados.analise_da_resposta()` is the pure function that turns the answer into the sides and caveats in force. Each side of the answer needs a position and at least one outlet of the pauta (tolerant comparison, `qa.mesmo_veiculo`). A generic actor is moved from `atores` to `grupos` in code (`lados.ator_generico`): one whose words are all in `lados.TERMOS_GENERICOS`, which has the unnamed collectives, the vague designations ("o governo", "a empresa") and, since 23/10/2026, institutions with no qualifier ("a campanha", "o ministério", "o tribunal", "o partido", "a coligação"), so "governo federal", "campanha de Lula" and "governo do Espírito Santo" stay actors; and one whose main word (the first that is not an article, a particle or a quantifier) is a collective of people in the closed list `lados.COLETIVOS_DE_PESSOAS`, even with a qualifier ("comunidade de programação", "usuários do fórum", "economistas do Ipea"), unless that word is capitalized, as a proper name ("Comunidade Europeia"). Since 29/10/2026 an unnamed source is a group too, even with the outlet that heard it ("uma fonte da Eurogamer", "pessoas próximas ao ministro", "interlocutores do governo"): the list has fonte, pessoa(s) and interlocutor(es) (the C3.52 of the blind validation, where "uma fonte da Eurogamer" made the "Sim" of an invented dispute). A side that came with groups only is a `Ressalva` (`grupos`, `posicao`, `veiculos`, `trecho`). If one of its groups is an actor of a side, by the name comparison of the QA (`qa.mesmo_ator`), it leaves, with a note in `ajustes`, and its excerpt, if found in the texts, goes to that side together with its outlets. Otherwise it only stays if its `trecho` is in the title or whole text of one of its outlets, with the same check as the excerpts of the actors (literal or near-literal); otherwise it is discarded, with the reason. A side that came with a named actor and lost them all in the check below is discarded and never becomes a caveat, even if it listed groups. A side with neither actor nor group is an invalid answer, unless it is the only side of the answer: then the pauta gets the neutral side at once, with no new attempt, which is the case of a purely informative story. In a side that has a named actor the groups are dropped, since they agree with it. At most `MAX_RESSALVAS` (2) caveats are kept, the first ones, with a warning in the log. The named sides must be at most 3, each with an actor that no other side has. The question only stays with two named sides or more, each with its own `resposta`, different from the others (compared without case, accents or punctuation); with one named side the question is dropped, and two or more named sides without a question are invalid. With no named side at all the pauta gets the neutral side (`lado_unico()`: every outlet, no actors, position "relato dos fatos, sem lados definidos") and its caveats are discarded, since there is no side for them to qualify; `Analise.descartadas` keeps every discarded caveat as a pair with its reason (no proven excerpt, more than 2 caveats, no named side). Every outlet of the pauta should be in some side or caveat of the answer as it came, before anything is discarded, and one outlet may report more than one side. An outlet left out of all of them (`lados._veiculos_sem_lado`, decision of 25/10/2026) enters the side when the analysis ends with a single named side, since that side is the whole story, and stays in the neutral side when there is no named side; with two sides or more, an outlet whose text (title and whole text) mentions no actor of any side, by the count of `mencoes`, enters as context (`Analise.contexto`, `veiculos_de_contexto` in the record): it stays in the pauta, out of the sides and of the block, and must still be in `fontes_citadas`; one whose text mentions an actor of a side still makes the answer invalid ("veículo da pauta em nenhum lado, com ator de um lado no texto"), since the answer left it out of that actor's side. Each case leaves a note in `ajustes`. Until then any outlet left out made the answer invalid, which in the 90+79+43 of the preview of 25/10 sent the pauta to the neutral side after two attempts that both had Zuckerberg as the single side with only the NeoFeed. Measured on the 243 recorded answers of the side analysis, the first attempts and the refused second ones included (`data/agrupamento/correcoes_20261025/medir_veiculo_fora.py`, 115 of pautas with several outlets): only those two had an outlet left out, both now give the single side of Zuckerberg with the three outlets, no other analysis changes, and the context case never happened. Actors are compared without accents, case, articles or particles (da, de, do, das, dos, e), and a name contained in another is the same actor ("Duarte" and "gestão de Duarte"), so it does not separate two sides. `Analise.ajustes` lists what the code changed in the answer.

With a question, the answer must also carry `respostas_por_ator`: for each named actor, its answer to the question and an excerpt copied literally from the text of an outlet of the pauta (an actor may come in more than one entry; every proven excerpt is kept). `_conferir_atores()` keeps in each side only the actors whose proven answer is the side's: the excerpt must have at least `MIN_TRECHO` characters, bring the actor's answer (see below) and be in the title or whole text of that outlet, even when the call read a cut of it, literally (spaces, quotation marks and case are normalised, and quotes or ellipses around it are ignored; the comparison is `recorte.literal()`) or near-literally (`recorte.localizar_trecho()`): compared word by word, ignoring punctuation, case and the kind of quotes, some contiguous stretch of the text may differ from it by at most one word in ten (replaced, added or missing; rounded down, so an excerpt of fewer than ten words must have the same words), and no difference may involve a negation (não, nem, nunca, jamais). An excerpt accepted near-literally is replaced by the stretch as it is in the outlet, which is what locates its paragraph in the cut by relevance, and `Evidencia.trecho_do_llm` keeps what the model wrote; the same check applies to the excerpts of the caveats. When the answer has two sides or more with a named actor (counted before the check), the excerpt must also bring the actor's answer; with a single side there is no dispute to invent, so the excerpt only has to be in the text, and an adjustment records it (since 24/10: on 23/10, the check took the only actor of the A14, whose excerpt was "a OpenAI admitiu..."). An excerpt brings the actor's answer (`lados.traz_a_resposta`, `Evidencia.prova`; with a dispute, an entry counts only when `Evidencia.vale`) when there is, in this order: a position verb of `ai/verbos.py` in the excerpt; or one in the sentence of the text where the excerpt is (`lados.frase_do_trecho`; the sentence is also split at ";", so in a title "A quer X; B defende Y" the verb of B does not count for the excerpt of A, the case of the N27 of 23/10); or the excerpt is a direct quote (between quotes, a curly quote opened in one paragraph staying open in the next, or a line of speech marked with a dash); or the sentence names the actor and has a decision verb (`verbos.FORMAS_DE_DECISAO`: determinar, decidir, suspender, mandar, participles included; a separate list, not position verbs; the QA uses only its participles, in the passive with the actor as agent), since whoever decides answers in favour of the decision; or an attribution marker in the sentence ("segundo ele", "de acordo com ela", "na avaliação dele"). Since 29/10/2026 (decisions of the owner after the blind validation) it also accepts, in this order after those: a position noun followed by the actor (`_SUBSTANTIVO_DE_POSICAO`: "a alegação de Flávio é a de que...", "a versão da emissora"), with the actor's name starting right after the noun (the run of words of the name, stopping at the first other word or at punctuation: "a acusação de improbidade contra a Construtora Alfa" does not count for the company), whole, or one word of it with the actor named in the sentence or the paragraph; a decision verb with the actor taken up by a role that the analysis gives it (`CARGOS`: "O ministro rejeitou..." for "ministro Ricardo Villas Bôas Cueva"), when the name, whole or by the surname, is in the same paragraph (`paragrafo_do_trecho`), the surname not shared by another person of the texts (`outro_com_o_sobrenome`) and the role in the sentence not followed by another person's name ("o ministro Gilmar Mendes rejeitou"); and the report of a cited outlet that is not an outlet of the pauta (`_veiculo_citado`: "divulgada pelo jornal Aftonbladet", "de acordo com o UOL", "segundo o X", "X publicou"), with the name right after the marker (only an article and the type of outlet in between: "de acordo com o Ministério Público, a Construtora Alfa...", or the ordinal "no segundo semestre", do not count for the company) or right before the active verb, for an actor that is not a person's name (`nome_de_pessoa`) unless the type of outlet comes right before it ("o jornal Aftonbladet"), since a person answers with a position verb (rule 2 of 29/10: a cited outlet that states the fact the actor denies is a side). The code cannot tell an outlet from another organisation, so "segundo o Banco Central" passes the same way. Rejeitar, proibir and acolher joined `verbos.FORMAS_DE_DECISAO`, with the participle of negar (already a position verb) for the passive; in the side analysis their participles count only with the actor as agent of the passive (`verbos.PARTICIPIOS_SO_COM_AGENTE`, `lados._decisoes_do_ator`: "o pedido foi negado pelo relator"), since "a construtora teve o pedido negado" names the loser. And a refusal to comment in the excerpt (`_RECUSA_DE_COMENTAR`: "não comentamos", "não discutimos", "não se pronunciou", "preferiu não comentar") brings no answer, even as a quote: in the excerpt, unless a position verb other than the reporting ones (dizer, informar, declarar, afirmar, responder, infinitives and compound past included) comes after the refusal ("não comentou o caso, mas negou..." still answers; "disse que não comenta", "limitou-se a dizer" and "a reportagem questionou a prefeitura, que não respondeu" do not); in the sentence, when the excerpt has no verb of its own and the rest of the sentence only reports the refusal. "Não respondeu" alone counts as a refusal, and so do "não vai se manifestar", "recusou-se a comentar" and "não quis comentar". The owner of the verb (`_verbo_do_outro_lado`, `_do_outro_lado`): a position or decision verb does not count for the actor when it belongs to an actor of the response on the other side, one with no answer on the actor's side (answers are compared by their first word when it is "Sim" or "Não", so "Não" and "Não, o texto foi fiel" are the same side; each actor keeps every answer of every entry, by its exact name first). The owner is the agent of a decision passive, that occurrence's own (`verbos.agente_da_passiva`); or the subject: the name right before the verb from the last comma of the clause (`_LIMITE_DA_ORACAO`), and, when another verb of the clause comes first, the name before that first verb ("A prefeitura questionou a construtora e disse...": the prefeitura said it); with the verb right after a comma, the name right after a verb of speech, up to "que" or punctuation ("..., diz ministro"; "..., disse Lula sobre a Meta"), or else the first name of the clause (the apposition: "A Construtora Alfa, em resposta à prefeitura, negou"; "O conselho, presidido por Edson Lacerda, determinou"); with the verb opening a clause after "que", the subject of the clause before ("A prefeitura, que questionou..."). Names are read by the words only one actor has (digits included, "TV4"), the role the analysis gives the actor and an acronym the paragraph writes in capitals ("MP Eleitoral"); a word the actor shares with an actor on the other side ("Bolsonaro" for Flávio and Jair), with no word only one of them has, is doubt, and in doubt the verb goes to the other side. A name that is not an actor of the response does not count ("a empresa afirmou" stays the company's), nor does an actor on the same side (the TV4 speaking through its programming director). The role path (`_pelo_cargo`) uses the same filtered verbs. The statement of the past (`fala_do_passado`): the verb of the statement (the first position verb of the sentence) in the past tense (`PRETERITOS`), with a mark of the past in the frame of the statement (`_moldura_da_fala`: what comes before the verb, out of quotes, and up to eight words after it, before "que", a comma or a colon; never the content of the statement: "afirmou que o contrato assinado em 2021 é válido" is a statement of now): a date of a year before the article's (`_DATA_DITA`, with "em" or "no ano de", the day and the month allowed; with the month, only more than three months before the article, so "em dezembro de 2025" in an article of January 2026 is recent; "desde 2018" never), "durante a pandemia", "à época", "no ano passado" (`_MARCA_DO_PASSADO`); or, in the sentence before it in the same paragraph, a mark that points to a time already told ("à época", "na época", "durante a pandemia", `_MARCA_ANAFORICA`; a year there is the recap of the case, not the time of the statement). A mark of the present in the sentence ("nesta terça", "nesta semana", "hoje", "ontem", "na sexta", "no domingo", `_MARCA_DO_PRESENTE`) makes it a statement of the new fact, whatever the sentence before says. A side left with no actor is discarded, as said above. A question that is not a yes-or-no one (starting with qual, quais, como, quem, o que, por que, onde or quando, `lados._QUESTAO_ABERTA`) leads to the neutral side, with no caveats and no new attempt, whatever the number of sides (second decision of 24/10/2026): the question in dispute is answered in opposite ways, different plans or priorities are not a dispute, and a single side with one of the actors, when the check removed the other, unbalances a story that compares two of them (the N27 of 24/10 kept only Flávio, and the analyst gave his plan two turns against one for Lula's). From the first decision of 24/10 until then, an open question invalidated only an answer with two named sides or more. Without a question nothing is checked and the actors stay as they came. `Analise.respostas` keeps every entry with the result of the check, and `Lado.trechos` the excerpts of the actors that stayed.

A side whose actors appear in at most one sentence of the texts that the generation receives is a thin side ("lado fino", `Lado.fino`; only in pautas with two sides or more, `lados_finos()`). The count is done by the code, `lados.mencoes()`, over the title and the sentences of each text as cut by relevance for the generation (`lados.com_mencoes()`, called by `Summarizer.preparar()` once the cut exists; the revisions keep that count), with the name rule of `qa.ator_citado` ("o prefeito" does not count for "Duarte"), and is kept in `Lado.mencoes`; the excerpts listed by the model do not enter it. Since the fourth decision of 24/10/2026 a mention that only appears inside quotation marks does not count (`lados.fora_das_aspas`, applied to each sentence by `_frases_fora_das_aspas`): another actor speaking of him ("“Seria prudente que o comandante...”, declarou" the minister, in the A79) and a name quoted as a title (the channel “Exército de Lula”, in the N13). Straight double quotes, curly double quotes (“ ”, « », „) and curly single quotes count; the straight single quote does not, since it is also an apostrophe, and a closing curly single quote with none open is an apostrophe ("Sant’Ana"); a curly double quote left open continues in the next paragraph only when that paragraph opens with quotes. The actor's own speech counts when the text names him outside the quotes ("“...”, afirmou Lula"); when his name is only inside his own quote (Lula's "Exército do Lula" in the N13), it does not, as with any sentence that names him only by his office. This is only for the count, not for the QA rule. Measured on the 61 recorded analyses with a dispute (`data/agrupamento/correcoes_20261024b/medir_sobrenomes.py`): 28 divisions of the budget and 3 thin sides change, and the surnames that start to count (Amorim, Cueva, Góes, Janones, Marques) are all of the right person; in the A79 the minister goes from 2 sentences to 4 and the split from 350 and 350 to 430 and 230. The sources carry little material for a thin side, so the block tells its analyst not to expand beyond what the sources carry, and the budget gives him a target in proportion to the mentions, at most 90 words per sentence of his side: 90 with one sentence, below the minimum of 120 of the other analysts (third decision of 24/10/2026). The QA minimum is at most `QA_MIN_LADO_FINO`; the cap of `QA_MAX_LADO_FINO` (200) is a diagnostic of `scripts/testar_episodio.py`.

There is at most one new attempt per analysis (`lados.analisar_lados()`). An invalid answer or a failed call gets it with the same input plus `pedido_de_correcao()`, which carries the validation error, the refused answer and a reminder to group the actors by their answer to the question; `Analise.primeira` keeps the refused attempt, and if the second attempt is not valid either, the pauta goes on with the neutral side and no caveats, with a warning in the log, and the episode has one analyst. A valid answer in which an actor is in a side with no entry in `respostas_por_ator`, or with entries none of which holds (`Analise.recusados`), gets it with `pedido_das_respostas()`, a single request that names the actors without an entry and gives, for each refused one, the excerpt and the reason, and asks for each the sentence, copied literally, in which the text brings the actor's answer: if the new answer is valid it is the one in force (an actor still without an entry or an excerpt that holds leaves, and `Analise.primeira` keeps the first answer); if it is not, the first answer stays, without the actor, and `Analise.segunda` keeps the refused one. It is the same single new attempt: after the correction of an invalid answer, a refused excerpt asks for nothing more. An open question gets no new attempt: it is the neutral side at once.

In a two-sided dispute the answers of the sides are "Sim" and "Não", one each (`lados.sim_e_nao`, case, accents and a final period ignored; third decision of 24/10/2026); with three sides it is enough that they differ. An answer that is otherwise valid and fails only this keeps its sides, with the reason in `Analise.sem_sim_e_nao` (not a problem), and gets the new attempt too, with `pedido_das_respostas()`: alone, ending with `LEMBRETE_DO_SIM_E_NAO`, or in the same request for missing entries or refused excerpts, adding `REGRA_DO_SIM_E_NAO`. It asks each side and each actor to answer "Sim" if its excerpt affirms what the question asks and "Não" if it denies it, and a question that cannot be answered so to be rewritten as a yes-or-no one about the same fact. The new answer is in force only if it is valid and brings "Sim" and "Não" in two sides; otherwise, for any reason (invalid, failed call, still descriptive, a single side), the first stays, and `Analise.segunda` keeps the refused one: this rule never brings an analysis down, unlike an invalid answer, which ends in the neutral side. When the first already had "Sim" and "Não" and only lacked an entry, the new answer is in force when valid, as before, even without them.
