# The word budget

> Moved out of `CLAUDE.md` on 04/11/2026, verbatim, so that the context every session and worker loads stays small. `CLAUDE.md` keeps a short summary under the same heading; when this file changes in a way that makes that summary wrong, fix the summary too.

The number of words of the script follows the material the generation receives (`ai/orcamento.py`, item 3 of the plan of 19/10/2026, adopted on 23/10/2026). `Summarizer.preparar()` computes it once, from the words of the cut texts (`orcamento.palavras`: the cut of each text, without the title) and the mentions of each side, keeps it in `Entrada.orcamento` (and `ultimo_diagnostico["orcamento"]`) and passes it to the block of the generation and of both revisions and to the QA:

- The total target is `FATOR` (1.75, to be calibrated in the regression) times those words, with a floor of `PISO_TOTAL` (300) and a ceiling at the target of before for the cast size (`ALVO_DE_ANTES`: 390, 360 and 285 per character with one, two and three analysts, the centres of 360-420, 320-400 and 260-310). Texts above about 450 words (one analyst) or 620 (two) get the budget of before.
- Helena gets the share of before, between 35% and 45% (`parte_da_helena`: 45% with one analyst, 35% with two or three). With one side the analyst gets the rest; with two or more the analysts split it in proportion to the mentions of their side (`Lado.mencoes`), each with at least `MIN_ANALISTA` (120) words. No character gets more than `MAX_ALVO` (430), below the 480 the prompts used to ask. With two sides or more, the target of each analyst is also at most `MAX_POR_FRASE_DO_LADO` (90) words per sentence of his side (zero counts as one; a side with no count has no cap), and this cap beats the minimum: with one sentence the target is 90 (third decision of 24/10/2026; across the runs since 23/10, the marks of the analysts with a dispute were 6,7 per thousand words up to 50 words per sentence of material, 6,8 from 50 to 90 and 19,8 above 90). What the cap removes leaves the total; Helena keeps her share of the total before it, up to 45% of the total that remains, and `Orcamento.reduzido` still looks only at the material of the pauta. Measured without the model (`data/agrupamento/correcoes_20261024c/medir_teto.py` and `conferir_teto_no_codigo.py`): 22 of the 65 recorded analyses with a dispute and 3 of the 20 items change, all by the right count (A79 430 and 230 → 360 and 180, N13 120 → 90, N92 120 → 90); one old analysis of the pauta 79+105 counts "Corpo de Bombeiros Militar" as the commander, a count error that makes its cap looser, not tighter. With the cap, Helena's turns get longer when the analysts have fewer (A79: 5 of 75 instead of 6 of 65). Targets are rounded to tens, half up.
- The plan of the turns (`Orcamento.plano`, a `Plano`; second decision of 24/10/2026) closes the arithmetic of every target. Each analyst gets the turns his target asks with at most `MAX_FALA_DO_ANALISTA` (90) words each, and at least `MIN_FALAS_DO_ANALISTA` (2). Helena opens and closes and speaks between the rounds: in each round, the analysts who still have a turn speak in the order of the sides. She has one turn more than the analyst who speaks most, and her target is divided among them. The size of each turn is rounded to 5. With one analyst of 430 and Helena with 350: Helena 6 turns of about 60, the analyst 5 of about 85, 11 turns; with two analysts of 360 and 120 (the N13): Helena 5 of 50, 4 of 90 and 2 of 60, in the order H 1 2 H 1 2 H 1 H 1 H. `Orcamento.turnos` is the length of the order. Until then the block asked a single number of turns (the total divided by 75) of 70 to 80 words for everyone: the model followed it in 7 of 8 items of the short run of 24/10, Helena, who speaks every other turn, got 49% of the words against the 35% to 45% of her target, and the analysts could not reach theirs. The plan of the 20 items of the regression, checked without the model (`data/agrupamento/correcoes_20261024b/planos.py`), always closes.
- The block gives each target as a single number (since 24/10; on 23/10 they were ranges of ±10%, and the model aimed at the floor of every range: the scripts came out with 66% of the target). The QA minimum is 60% of the target (`minimo_do_qa`; 75% until the fourth decision of 24/10), never above the minimum of before, so it is always below the target, and the difference is a margin; the maximums are those of before. The QA message of a character or total outside its limits says what is accepted and the target of the block, and both revisions aim at the target of the block, not at the middle of the accepted range, expanding with what the texts carry, with no new argument.

The prompts no longer hard-code word, turn or thin-side numbers: `PROMPT_SISTEMA`, `_REGRAS_DO_ROTEIRO`, `LEMBRETE` and `LEMBRETE_DO_ROTEIRO` point to the block, and `PROMPT_SISTEMA` tells the model to count the words of each character and expand the turns to the target with what the texts carry, with no new argument (the instruction to expand, which the scripts of 17/10 followed with turns of 96 words, had left the prompt on 23/10). With the sections of 23 and 24/10, `PROMPT_SISTEMA` grew from 7,843 to 9,604 characters, and the plan adds about 200 characters to the block (about 450 with one side, up to about 900 with two). On a single source the generation comes after the side analysis, which measures the proportion of the text of the item, and with the fixed part counted in exact tokens the ceiling of 5,000 characters of the principal binds before the window for every single-source item of the simulations of 25/10 (the lowest common proportion, 3.63, leaves about 7,600 characters). With several outlets the window binds; under the rule of before 29/10 the generation lost up to about 1,960 characters of text against the prompt of before 23/10 (1,761 from the prompt and about 200 from the plan in the block). Changing `QA_*` in `.env` only changes the caps of the minimums and the maximums. The 8 to 9 sentences of the summary are still in `PROMPT_SISTEMA` and `PROMPT_RESUMO`. The pieces that change together are `ai/orcamento.py`, `lados._alvo_do_elenco()` with `reserva_do_bloco()`, `qa.alvo_de_cada_personagem()`, `qa.limites_de_cada_personagem()`, `qa.limites_do_total()` and the prose about the block in the prompts.
