# The input budget

> Moved out of `CLAUDE.md` on 04/11/2026, verbatim, so that the context every session and worker loads stays small. `CLAUDE.md` keeps a short summary under the same heading; when this file changes in a way that makes that summary wrong, fix the summary too.

`summarizer.montar_entrada()` builds the message for the episode and keeps it inside a character limit, so the provider never has to truncate it:

- Headers, the editor's notice (`AVISO DO EDITOR-CHEFE`), the block of sides (`LADOS DA PAUTA`) and the reminder are never cut. The budget sets aside for the block the size it really has. The one exception is the generation input, built before the thin sides and the word budget are known (both come from the cut itself): there the reserve is `lados.reserva_do_bloco()`, the size of the block with every side thin and the budget written with the longest numbers and the notice of little material, capped at `MAX_BLOCO`, which costs up to about 250 characters. Since the final input uses the same reserve, it carries the same cut that was counted. The texts share what is left: each keeps its ceiling (5000 characters for the principal, 3000 for each of up to 2 related articles); if they do not fit, the principal gets about half and the related ones split the rest, and unused share is passed on. A related article that would get less than 400 characters is dropped, and the notice only counts and names what was actually included.
- Within its share, each text is cut by relevance (`recorte.recortar()`), in every call that carries source text: the generation, the revision of the script and the full revision, each with its own space. What enters, in this order of priority: the lide (`recorte.lide()`: the first paragraphs until they add up to 400 characters, skipping lines shorter than 60 characters, lines that look like a photo caption or a site call, such as "Ouça este conteúdo", "Compartilhar matéria" and "... — Foto: ...", and a line that repeats the title); the paragraphs that contain the evidence excerpts of the sides and of the caveats (`lados.trechos_de_evidencia()`, one excerpt of each side and caveat at a time, so no side is starved when space is short; an excerpt is searched in every text of the pauta); and the rest of the text, in order, up to the first paragraph that does not fit. A paragraph is a non-empty line. What enters is shown in the order of the text, with `[...]` where something was skipped, so the chronology is kept. With no excerpts and no skipped lines the cut is the beginning of the text ending at a paragraph end (a text longer than its ceiling used to be cut at exactly 5000 or 3000 characters, mid-sentence; it now ends at the last whole paragraph). `Entrada.recortes` says, per source, which paragraphs entered and why (`lide`, `trecho do lado N`, `trecho da ressalva N`, `ordem do texto`) and which paragraphs with an excerpt were left out; those are also logged as a warning.
- The limit comes from `Summarizer._limite_da_entrada()` (owner's decisions of 29/10/2026). With Ollama it is `OLLAMA_NUM_CTX` minus the output reserve, a margin (`MARGEM_TOKENS`, 256) and the fixed part of the call in exact tokens, converted to characters by 90% (`FOLGA_DA_MEDIDA`) of the proportion of the text of the item. The reserve is the smaller of `OLLAMA_NUM_PREDICT` and `LLM_RESERVA_SAIDA`, because `num_predict` may be larger than the window itself; a call that limits its own output reserves only that (the side analysis reserves `MAX_SAIDA_LADOS`, 1200 tokens). With an OpenAI-compatible provider the limit is `LLM_MAX_PROMPT_CHARS`; if a message still exceeds it, `OpenAIProvider` cuts it and logs an error.
- The fixed part is the system prompt with the chat template: counted once, with an empty user message and `num_predict` 1 (`OllamaProvider.contar_tokens_fixos`, through the machine brakes), and kept in `TOKENS_FIXOS` (`~/.local/state/techdigest/tokens_fixos.json`, next to the model lock, shared by the main folder, the worktree and the simulations) under the sha256 of the model, `OLLAMA_THINK` and the prompt (`ai/janela.py`), counted before the first call that uses the prompt (so the server never has its prefix in cache), and a count above 8 characters per token is refused; a new prompt, another model or another think gets a new count, while a model updated under the same name is not noticed (delete the file then). Counted on 29/10: `PROMPT_SISTEMA` 2,591 tokens, `PROMPT_LADOS` 1,573, `PROMPT_REVISOR` 1,124, `PROMPT_ROTEIRO` 1,041, `PROMPT_TITULO` 335, `PROMPT_RESUMO` 242, `PROMPT_AGRUPADOR` 879, the template alone 18. A provider that does not count converts the prompt by 3.0 characters per token (`CHARS_POR_TOKEN_DO_PROMPT`).
- The proportion is that of the text: the characters of the user message per token, without the fixed part, measured from `provider.ultimo_uso` after every call of the item (the lowest measure of the item counts; a call with no count changes nothing). It is per item: `Summarizer.preparar` resets it, so the texts of one item never change the limit of another, and the first call of each item (the side analysis) starts from `PROPORCAO_TIPICA` (4.3, the median of the 64 items of the simulations of 25/10, from 2.30 to 4.79). Until 29/10 the proportion was one for the whole run, the lowest measure of every call including the system prompt: in the second run with the grouping on of 25/10 a pauta of tables of votes measured 2.6 (2.34 with the margin), and every item after it went to generation with 206 to 966 characters of text, nine of them approved with little more than the lide. Measured on those three simulations (`data/agrupamento/correcoes_20261029/medir_janela.py`): the 60 single-source items all get the ceiling (52 did), the multi-source ones get much more text (the pauta of Zanin from 206, 0 and 0 characters to 2,751, 1,375 and 1,046), and with the typical proportion one first call goes over its reserve (the tables, 7,250 tokens) and none over the window.
- No call is cut silently. Every request to Ollama goes with `"truncate": false` and `"shift": false`, through a small HTTP client (`llm._ClienteOllama`; the Python client 0.6 sends neither; `ps` and `generate` are still its own). An input larger than the window comes back at once with HTTP 400 and the exact count (`JanelaExcedida`, with `tokens` and `janela`), instead of Ollama cutting the middle of the prompt (which used to take the system prompt with it and was only in its log); an answer that reaches the end of the window stops with `done_reason` "length", which the provider already raises, instead of shifting the context. `shift` is an option of the model's server: switching it between requests reloads the model (about 10 s each, checked on 29/10), so it is the same in every request. A call that goes over the window is redone once (`Summarizer._na_janela`, and the generation in `process`), with the message rebuilt by the proportion the error measured (or, if the error has no count, the lowest of the run, or 3.0; if that would not shrink the input, 80% of the proportion of the item). The first attempt of the side analysis lets `JanelaExcedida` through (`lados._tentar`), so the whole analysis is redone; a window error of its new attempt counts as a failed call and does not bring down a first answer that held. A second window error is an error of the item, and so is a window error in a correction: it is not a rejection by the QA and does not count for the attempt limit.
- When the window, and not the ceiling, cuts the text of a single-source item, the log gets `Janela: o texto de <outlet> entrou na geração com N de M caracteres...` and the record of the run `aviso_da_janela` (`summarizer.aviso_da_janela`, from `Entrada.pela_janela`); every cut of the record says `cortado_pela_janela`, and each item its `proporcao_do_texto`. With several outlets the window is what shares the space, so there is no notice.
- The full revision carries the original episode too, so its source text is rebuilt with what is left of the window and can be much shorter than in the generation call. The revision of the script carries only the script, so it leaves more room for the sources. Both redo the cut by relevance for their own space and carry the same block of sides, with the thin sides as counted for the generation. The correction of the summary reuses the excerpts of the generation call, unchanged.
- The side analysis does not share this budget: it reads the whole texts (the principal and up to 2 related articles) in the space of its own call. Only if they do not fit are they cut, each in proportion to its size and at a paragraph end, with a warning in the log and the cut in `Analise.cortes`; the new attempt, which carries the correction request, reads less text in that case. The limit of the generation input is computed after the side analysis, so it already uses the proportion that call measured on the texts of the item.
